We don’t solve ordinary business problems

We're a systematic design studio built by practitioners who've shipped inside real enterprise constraints - not just presented concepts in a pitch deck.

Why it matters

We didn't set out to build an agency.
We set out to solve a problem we kept running into.

Too many design partners hand off files and disappear before the metric moves. We started UXUI Studio to close that gap - accountable from the first sketch to the shipped, measured result.

The beginning

Built in the halls of India's most demanding institute.

Founded by Deepak Singh, who spent a decade in enterprise, public-sector, and startup product design roles. The team he's built is led by IIT Bombay and IIT Delhi alumni with over a decade at Google, McKinsey & Company, and Samsung.

The conviction

Design is not decoration. It's one of the
highest - leverage investments a business can make.

An interface doesn't just look thoughtful - it changes what a customer does next. We hold every design decision to the same scrutiny a CFO applies to any other line item, because that's the standard our clients hold everything else to.

The scope

One system, applied end to end - not a service menu.

We don't hand you off between departments. The same team that shapes your strategy designs the interface, builds the design system, and ships the code - so nothing gets lost along the way.

The future

At the edge of what design can do next.

AI is changing what's possible inside a product - and inside our own process. We're building the discipline to use it well: faster research, faster iteration, judgment that stays firmly human.

Real clients.
Real stakes.

Bring us the problem
you haven't solved yet.

If it's ambitious, overdue, or stuck in committee -
that's usually where we do our best work.
